NOTEPD for Windows

Your notes.
Your computers.

A Windows notebook for writing, drawing, pictures and code, with local backups and optional Google Drive synchronization.

Google Drive is optional

Sign in with your own Google account to store your NOTEPD workspace in your Drive. Use the same account on another computer to retrieve your notes. Google controls account consent and permissions.

NOTEPD requests access to files it creates or you explicitly select. Your note content goes directly between the application and Google Drive; there is no NOTEPD cloud server.

Drive storage and API quotas apply. No paid NOTEPD synchronization service is required.

Keep writing offline

Your local notes and backups remain on your computer. Cloud changes synchronize while NOTEPD is running and connected. Keep independent backups of important documents.
