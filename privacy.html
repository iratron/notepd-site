Privacy policy

Effective November 18, 2026. Publisher: Ira Miller. Contact: irawmiller@gmail.com.

Local notes

NOTEPD stores notes, document history, preferences, profile images and backups on your computer in locations selected by you or the application. Its bundled spelling and grammar review runs locally.

Optional Google Drive access

If you connect Google Drive, NOTEPD requests the drive.file permission. This allows access to Drive files the app creates or you explicitly open or select for it. NOTEPD uses that access to upload, download and synchronize your notebook workspace, including text, images, drawings, comments, document history and saved tabs. It also reads the identifiers, names and versions needed to find and update those files.

For shared editing, NOTEPD synchronizes your profile display name, device identifier, active note identifier, paragraph, caret position and recent activity time. Other people who can access the same selected workspace may see those details. Google Drive sharing determines who can access the files.

How information is used and shared

Note content is transferred directly between your computer and Google Drive over HTTPS. NOTEPD does not operate a server that receives your notes. It does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train artificial-intelligence models. NOTEPD's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Sign-in security

Google handles your password and account consent. NOTEPD receives authorization tokens, not your Google password. Refresh tokens are encrypted for your Windows user account and stored separately from note backups. Legacy Windows Credential Manager tokens may be migrated to that protected storage. Tokens are not included in notebook exports or Drive snapshots.

Retention and control

Local backups and Google Drive files remain until removed by you or the app's backup-retention features. Signing out removes the local stored sign-in token; it does not delete your Drive files or revoke Google's account grant. You can revoke access in your Google Account's third-party connections settings, and remove cloud files in Google Drive. Uninstalling NOTEPD preserves notes and backups. Backups and synced workspaces are not end-to-end encrypted; protect your Windows and Google accounts.

Other services

Opening web links or using Google services is subject to those services' privacy policies. This static publisher website does not add advertising, analytics or tracking scripts; its hosting provider may process ordinary web-request information.

Changes and questions

Updates to this policy will be posted here with a revised effective date. Contact the publisher at the email above with privacy questions.
